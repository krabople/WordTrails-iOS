# Word Trails Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026. Word Trails is provided by Benjamin David Berger.

## Solo play

Word Trails does not collect or transmit your personal solo progress to the developer. Completed routes, statistics, gardens, achievements and reminder preferences are stored on your device. There is no developer account system or developer-operated game database, advertising, tracking or analytics SDK. Solo progress does not sync between devices. Removing the app may remove its locally saved progress; your device's normal backup settings may also apply.

## Optional Apple services

Co-op uses Apple Game Center. If you choose it, Game Center manages your player identity, friends, invitations, turn-based match data and associated notifications. Match data is shared with your Game Center partner through Apple's service. Apple processes information under its own [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer does not operate a separate server for these matches or upload your contacts.

Daily reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. You choose their time and can disable them in the app or in system Settings.

## Sharing and support

Sharing a result is your choice. The app passes the result card and app link to the destination you select in Apple's share sheet. That destination's own privacy practices apply.

If you voluntarily submit a support request, we use the information you provide to understand and respond to the request. [GitHub support issues](https://github.com/krabople/WordTrails-iOS/issues) are public: do not post private information, passwords or personal screenshots there. GitHub handles information under its own privacy policy.

## Changes and questions

This page will be updated if the app's privacy practices change. For questions, visit [Word Trails Support](SUPPORT.md). No user data is sold or used for advertising.
