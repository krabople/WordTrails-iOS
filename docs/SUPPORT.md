# Word Trails Support

Word Trails is a free daily word-ladder game for iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 17 or later.

## Get help

[Report a problem or ask a question](https://github.com/krabople/WordTrails-iOS/issues/new). Include your app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Issues are public; please do not include personal details or private screenshots.

## Playing a trail

Change one letter at a time to turn the starting word into the target. Each move must make a real word, and words cannot repeat within the same route. Hints and undo are available in solo play. Each difficulty has a new daily puzzle at midnight UTC, shared by all players.

Tap a completed daily trail to view and share the saved attempt. Replay starts a separate attempt. Completed trails are also available from your history. Personal progress is stored on this device and does not sync between iPhone and iPad.

## Reminders and co-op

Enable an optional daily reminder and choose its time in Settings. If it does not arrive, check Word Trails notification permissions and Focus settings in iOS Settings.

Co-op is optional and uses Apple Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in device Settings, then invite a friend from the Co-op tab. Solo play requires neither an account nor internet access.
